About Trickopedia

Every move.
One library.

Trickopedia is the ultimate body movement database: one place to find, learn and share moves, from your first cartwheel to a cheat 720, or from your first push-up to a full planche.

Tricking, breaking, parkour and freerunning clips are scattered across a dozen apps. We collect them in one simple, searchable library, sorted by category and level, so you can go straight to the move you want to learn.
